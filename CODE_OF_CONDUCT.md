# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We welcome respectful, constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Contributors are expected to communicate professionally and help maintain a safe, inclusive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate.
- Offer actionable, constructive feedback.
- Respect differing perspectives and experience levels.
- Protect private information and follow responsible security disclosure practices.
- Keep examples, tests, and demonstrations authorized and safe.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publishing private information without permission, or encouraging unauthorized access to systems are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer using an available private contact channel. Do not post personal or sensitive information in public issues. The maintainer may remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation as appropriate. No guaranteed response time is promised.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related community interactions.
