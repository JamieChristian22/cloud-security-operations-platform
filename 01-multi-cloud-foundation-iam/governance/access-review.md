# Quarterly Access Review
1. Export active users, groups, privileged roles, service identities, and last sign-in/key-use timestamps.
2. Flag terminated identities, 45+ day inactive workforce accounts, 90+ day stale service credentials, and direct privileged assignments.
3. Send each role owner an attestation list.
4. Remove unapproved access within one business day for privileged roles and three business days for standard roles.
5. Record reviewer, decision, date, ticket, and evidence.
6. Re-run the audit and document residual exceptions with expiration dates.
